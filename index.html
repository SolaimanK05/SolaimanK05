01 / about

I teach machines how to decide.

I'm a Computer Science & Engineering undergrad at Islamic University of Technology (IUT), Bangladesh, and part of our robotics team, Blueprint Robotics. On the team I'm the software guy. I write the control algorithms, tune the logic, and spend a lot of time figuring out why the robot turned left when it very clearly should have turned right.

Together we've built a line follower, a robo-soccer squad, an RC car, a nano battle bot, and a self-driving car for WRO Future Engineers. The algorithms behind them are usually my part. I'm also a bit hooked on hackathons (the caffeine, the chaos, the 3 a.m. breakthroughs) and I build apps on the side with React, Next.js, and Supabase.
